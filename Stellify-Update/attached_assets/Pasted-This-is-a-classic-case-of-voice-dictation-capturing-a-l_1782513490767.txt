💡 Why this structure works for Replit:
Schema First: It forces Replit to build the database columns for your new dates and flags before it tries to build the UI, preventing nasty crash errors.
Explicit Routing: It gives Replit clear logic on how to handle the Pastor Approval workflow, which is complex and usually requires specific backend logic to execute properly.
Dropdown Definitions: It explicitly lists out your event types and routing options so the AI doesn't have to guess what you meant by "different types of events."
